Explicit Consent Statement

Special Category Data · Last updated: October 2026

Declaration of explicit consent regarding health (menstrual cycle / medication) data, sensitive location data, and push notification flows.

1. NATURE AND SCOPE OF EXPLICIT CONSENT

Under applicable data protection laws, I freely and knowingly provide my explicit consent for the processing and couple-synchronized sharing of my personal and special-category personal data as detailed below, in full accordance with the principles set forth in the Clarification Text.

2. DATA CATEGORIES SUBJECT TO EXPLICIT CONSENT AND PURPOSES

• Special Category Data (Health Information): Dates, cycle lengths, symptom notes, and medication schedules entered into the cycle and medicine tracker modules, processed to forecast menstrual cycles, generate reminders, and display synchronizations on my paired partner's screen, • Geolocation Data: Coordinates selected for meeting points and GPS locations tagged onto photos, processed to display shared map markers and facilitate route planning, • Shared Couple Content: Memory photos, love letters, and mood statuses stored in encrypted form strictly for sharing with my paired partner, • Push Notifications: Receiving instant notifications for approaching anniversaries, delivered letters, and medication schedules.

3. WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

I understand and agree that I may withdraw this consent at any time by disabling health or location permissions within the app settings, or by requesting account deletion.
