Personal Data Protection & GDPR Clarification Text

GDPR & KVKK Compliant · Last updated: November 2026

Information on the processing purposes, collection methods, legal grounds of your personal data, and your rights under GDPR and Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Data Controller: NECATI TASCI (CoupleSky) · Contact & support: support@couplesky.app. As CoupleSky ("App" or "Data Controller"), under the relevant data protection legislations (GDPR and Turkish Law No. 6698), we place the highest priority on the security and privacy of our users' personal data. This Clarification Text explains the purposes for which your data collected via our mobile app is processed and outlines your statutory rights.

2. CATEGORIES OF PROCESSED PERSONAL DATA AND DATA TYPES

Within the scope of our shared couple journal, time-capsule sealed letters, location-based date planner, and health/menstrual cycle tracking features, the following personal data is processed: • Identity and Contact Information: Full name, username, email address, gender selection. • Couple and Relationship Information: Pairing code, first meeting date, relationship anniversary dates. • Visual and Audio Records: Photos uploaded to the shared gallery or private journals, memory pictures, and profile avatars. • Location Information: Meeting point coordinates and optional GPS pins attached to memory photos. • Special Category Personal Data (Health Information): Menstrual cycle start dates, predicted cycle intervals, daily medication/vitamin hours and adherence logs. • Messaging and Memory Content: Time-capsule letters, private daily notes, and mood selections (stored encrypted on our servers). • Transaction and Financial Information: Anonymous transaction identifiers, subscription status, package type, and token balance processed via RevenueCat and app stores (App Store / Google Play). • Device and Operation Security: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, unique device identifier (UUID), last login timestamp, security logs, and IP address used to rate-limit sign-in / password-reset attempts (kept for at most 1 day). • Error Logs: When the app errors, the error message, technical stack trace, app version, platform (iOS/Android) and your account identifier. Content such as letters or journal entries is not written to these logs; they are kept for at most 30 days. • Advertising Data: Non-personalized advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob for displayed in-app banners.

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING PERSONAL DATA

Your personal data is processed in accordance with applicable data protection principles for the following purposes: • Account creation, secure authentication, and prevention of unauthorized access, • Connecting two partners via a unique private pairing code to enable real-time synchronized memory and calendar sharing, • Cryptographic encryption of time-locked letters and preserving their confidentiality until the chosen unlock date, • Displaying shared date locations on map with convenient routing, • Delivering timely push notifications for medicine schedules, cycle updates, anniversaries, and unlocked letters, • Provisioning CoupleSky Premium entitlements so that a single subscription covers both partners, • Maintaining network and information security against cyber attacks and brute-force attempts.

4. PARTIES TO WHOM PERSONAL DATA IS TRANSFERRED

Your personal data is never sold to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Data is shared strictly to perform our services with the following vetted providers, some of which are located internationally: • Supabase Inc.: Secure PostgreSQL database, encrypted cloud storage, and authentication infrastructure (data is hosted in the Frankfurt / Germany region), • RevenueCat Inc.: In-app purchases, subscription entitlement verification, and receipt validation, • Google Cloud / Firebase, Apple (APNs) & Expo Notifications: Delivery of push notifications to your device (notification text may include health details such as medicine name or cycle status that your partner shares, and passes through these services), • Google LLC (AdMob & Google Maps): Map services and non-personalized advertising, • Apple Inc. & Google LLC: Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google authentication services; on-device address search (the address text you type when choosing a location), • Expo (Expo Updates / EAS): Distribution of app updates; your IP address, platform and app version are sent when checking for updates, • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): Used as a fallback when the device's own address search returns nothing; only the text you type in the address search box and your IP address are sent, • Unsplash: Sample images shown in explore (demo) mode; your IP address is visible while the images load, • Competent Public Authorities: In response to lawful court orders or binding legal obligations. Basis for transfers abroad: Transfers to providers located abroad are made under Article 9 of KVKK, relying on an adequacy decision of the Personal Data Protection Board, or, where there is none, on the appropriate safeguards listed in Article 9 (standard contract, binding corporate rules, etc.) or other cases provided by law. You can ask which basis is used for which provider at support@couplesky.app.

5. DATA COLLECTION METHODS AND LEGAL GROUNDS

Your data is collected electronically via app UI inputs, camera/gallery permissions, device sensors, and notification permissions. Legal grounds include: • Contractual Necessity: Performance of the User Agreement and core service delivery, • Legitimate Interests: Enhancing app security, preventing fraud, and optimizing stability without overriding fundamental user rights, • Explicit Consent (KVKK Art. 6/2): For health-related cycle/medicine data, given with a separate tick box when you first use the feature, apart from the terms acceptance at sign-up. Location depends on your granting the device location permission and choosing to use the related feature.

6. DATA SECURITY AND ENCRYPTION (AES-256)

CoupleSky writes your daily notes and letters to the database encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is derived inside the app from a random, couple-specific value kept on the server. This is therefore not end-to-end encryption: as the party operating the service we are technically able to access this content, and we do not use it for any other purpose, including marketing. Access is limited by Row-Level Security (RLS) rules to authenticated members of the relevant couple. Your shared photos are kept in private storage and displayed only through time-limited, signed links; your profile photo (avatar), however, is kept in public storage at an unguessable address and can be viewed by anyone who has that address. All traffic is encrypted with TLS.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION (RIGHT TO BE FORGOTTEN)

You can request permanent account deletion anytime from Profile > Account Management & Danger Zone. Deletion requests enter a 7-day safety grace period during which you can cancel by logging back in. After 7 days, all profile records, private journals, letters, and storage files are permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers. Shared memories are wiped for both sides, uncoupling your partner. Your partner is informed through an in-app explanation and a notification when you request deletion, when you cancel it, and when it is carried out. Backup copies are purged within 30 days. Under GDPR Article 17 and applicable data protection legislation, the statutory right to delete your personal data, uploaded photos, and letters cannot be conditioned upon an active paid subscription. Users may permanently delete their own content or terminate their accounts at any time, even on the free tier. Retention periods: • Account, profile and shared content: until your account is deleted (deleted 7 days after the request), • Star registration and coordinate: retained for the couple during a 14-day Stellar Hibernation (Grace Period) following subscription expiration; if not renewed within 14 days, the coordinate is released to the public catalog for other couples to claim, • Star memory vault and planet data: securely archived in the system vault for 90 days after coordinate release; if renewed within 90 days, restored to the original or a newly chosen star, • Media storage purge: high-resolution photo files of accounts that remain expired for 90 days are permanently purged from cloud storage for optimization purposes, following a 7-day advance notice (text records may be retained), • Error logs: at most 30 days, • Sign-in and password-reset attempt records (including IP address): at most 1 day, • Records that prevent duplicate notifications: 14 days, • In-app account notices shown to your partner: 30 days after they are seen, • Push notification token: for the life of your session and account, • Records held by third-party providers (e.g. RevenueCat, Expo, Google): subject to the provider's own retention policies.

8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER GDPR AND DATA PROTECTION LAWS

You have the right at any time to: a) Inquire whether your personal data is being processed, b) Request information regarding the processing of your data, c) Understand the purpose of processing and verify compliance, d) Know third parties to whom data has been transferred domestically or abroad, e) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, f) Request deletion or erasure of your personal data under statutory conditions, g) Request notification of corrections and deletions to third-party recipients, h) Object to decisions made solely by automated systems that cause an adverse legal effect, i) Seek compensation if you suffer damages due to unlawful processing. To exercise your rights, you can reach us through in-app support or directly via email at support@couplesky.app.
