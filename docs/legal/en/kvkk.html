Personal Data Protection & GDPR Clarification Text

GDPR & KVKK Compliant · Last updated: October 2026

Information on the processing purposes, collection methods, legal grounds of your personal data, and your rights under GDPR and Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Data Controller: NECATI TASCI (CoupleSky) · Contact & support: support@couplesky.app. As CoupleSky ("App" or "Data Controller"), under the relevant data protection legislations (GDPR and Turkish Law No. 6698), we place the highest priority on the security and privacy of our users' personal data. This Clarification Text explains the purposes for which your data collected via our mobile app is processed and outlines your statutory rights.

2. CATEGORIES OF PROCESSED PERSONAL DATA AND DATA TYPES

Within the scope of our shared couple journal, time-capsule sealed letters, location-based date planner, and health/menstrual cycle tracking features, the following personal data is processed: • Identity and Contact Information: Full name, username, email address, gender selection. • Couple and Relationship Information: Pairing code, first meeting date, relationship anniversary dates. • Visual and Audio Records: Photos uploaded to the shared gallery or private journals, memory pictures, and profile avatars. • Location Information: Meeting point coordinates and optional GPS pins attached to memory photos. • Special Category Personal Data (Health Information): Menstrual cycle start dates, predicted cycle intervals, daily medication/vitamin hours and adherence logs. • Messaging and Memory Content: Time-capsule letters, private daily notes, and mood selections (stored encrypted on our servers). • Transaction and Financial Information: Anonymous transaction identifiers, subscription status, package type, and token balance processed via RevenueCat and app stores (App Store / Google Play). • Device and Operation Security: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, unique device identifier (UUID), last login timestamp, and security logs. • Advertising Data: Non-personalized advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob for displayed in-app banners.

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING PERSONAL DATA

Your personal data is processed in accordance with applicable data protection principles for the following purposes: • Account creation, secure authentication, and prevention of unauthorized access, • Connecting two partners via a unique private pairing code to enable real-time synchronized memory and calendar sharing, • Cryptographic encryption of time-locked letters and preserving their confidentiality until the chosen unlock date, • Displaying shared date locations on map with convenient routing, • Delivering timely push notifications for medicine schedules, cycle updates, anniversaries, and unlocked letters, • Provisioning CoupleSky Premium entitlements so that a single subscription covers both partners, • Maintaining network and information security against cyber attacks and brute-force attempts.

4. PARTIES TO WHOM PERSONAL DATA IS TRANSFERRED

Your personal data is never sold to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Data is shared strictly to perform our services with the following vetted providers, some of which are located internationally: • Supabase Inc.: Secure PostgreSQL database, encrypted cloud storage, and authentication infrastructure, • RevenueCat Inc.: In-app purchases, subscription entitlement verification, and receipt validation, • Google Cloud / Firebase & Expo Notifications: Reliable delivery of push notifications to your device, • Google LLC (AdMob & Google Maps): Map services and non-personalized advertising, • Apple Inc. & Google LLC: Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google authentication services, • Competent Public Authorities: In response to lawful court orders or binding legal obligations.

5. DATA COLLECTION METHODS AND LEGAL GROUNDS

Your data is collected electronically via app UI inputs, camera/gallery permissions, device sensors, and notification permissions. Legal grounds include: • Contractual Necessity: Performance of the User Agreement and core service delivery, • Legitimate Interests: Enhancing app security, preventing fraud, and optimizing stability without overriding fundamental user rights, • Explicit Consent: For health-related cycle/medicine logs, sensitive location pins, and marketing communications.

6. DATA SECURITY AND ENCRYPTION (AES-256)

CoupleSky writes your daily notes and letters to the database encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is derived inside the app from a random, couple-specific value kept on the server. This is therefore not end-to-end encryption: as the party operating the service we are technically able to access this content, and we do not use it for any other purpose, including marketing. Access is limited by Row-Level Security (RLS) rules to authenticated members of the relevant couple. Your photos are kept in private storage and displayed only through time-limited, signed links. All traffic is encrypted with TLS.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION (RIGHT TO BE FORGOTTEN)

You can request permanent account deletion anytime from Profile > Account Management & Danger Zone. Deletion requests enter a 7-day safety grace period during which you can cancel by logging back in. After 7 days, all profile records, private journals, letters, and storage files are permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers. Shared memories are wiped for both sides, uncoupling your partner. Backup copies are purged within 30 days.

8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER GDPR AND DATA PROTECTION LAWS

You have the right at any time to: a) Inquire whether your personal data is being processed, b) Request information regarding the processing of your data, c) Understand the purpose of processing and verify compliance, d) Know third parties to whom data has been transferred domestically or abroad, e) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, f) Request deletion or erasure of your personal data under statutory conditions, g) Request notification of corrections and deletions to third-party recipients, h) Object to decisions made solely by automated systems that cause an adverse legal effect, i) Seek compensation if you suffer damages due to unlawful processing. To exercise your rights, you can reach us through in-app support or directly via email at support@couplesky.app.
