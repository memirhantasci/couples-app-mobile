Personal Data Protection & GDPR Clarification Text

GDPR & KVKK Compliant · Last updated: November 2026

Information on the processing purposes, collection methods, legal grounds of your personal data, and your rights under GDPR and Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Data Controller: NECATI TASCI (CoupleSky) · Address: Küçükbakkalköy Mah., Sümer Sok. No:8, Ataşehir, Istanbul, Türkiye · Contact & support: support@couplesky.app. As CoupleSky ("App" or "Data Controller"), under the relevant data protection legislations (GDPR and Turkish Law No. 6698), we place the highest priority on the security and privacy of our users' personal data. This Clarification Text explains the purposes for which your data collected via our mobile app is processed and outlines your statutory rights.

2. CATEGORIES OF PROCESSED PERSONAL DATA AND DATA TYPES

Within the scope of our shared couple journal, time-capsule sealed letters, location-based date planner, and health/menstrual cycle tracking features, the following personal data is processed: • Identity and Contact Information: Full name, username, email address, gender selection. • Couple and Relationship Information: Pairing code, first meeting date, relationship anniversary dates. When a relationship ends (for example when your partner deletes their account), the meeting and relationship dates are reset and entered again in a new pairing. • Visual and Audio Records: Photos uploaded to the shared gallery or private journals, memory pictures, and profile avatars. • Location Information: Meeting point coordinates and optional GPS pins attached to memory photos. • Special Category Personal Data (Health Information): Menstrual cycle start dates, predicted cycle intervals, daily medication/vitamin hours and adherence logs. • Messaging and Memory Content: Time-capsule letters, private daily notes, and mood selections (stored encrypted on our servers). • Transaction and Financial Information: Anonymous transaction identifiers, subscription status, package type, and token balance processed via RevenueCat and app stores (App Store / Google Play). • Device and Operation Security: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, unique device identifier (UUID), last sign-in and last app usage time, security logs, and IP address used to rate-limit sign-in / password-reset attempts (kept for at most 1 day). • Error Logs: When the app errors, the error message, technical stack trace, app version, platform (iOS/Android) and your account identifier. Content such as letters or journal entries is not written to these logs; they are kept for at most 30 days. • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data processed by Google AdMob for the rewarded ads shown in the app (limited to non-personalized ad requests). The app does not track you across other apps and websites, and no tracking permission (ATT) is requested.

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING PERSONAL DATA

Your personal data is processed in accordance with applicable data protection principles for the following purposes: • Account creation, secure authentication, and prevention of unauthorized access, • Linking two partners with a private pairing code and sharing the memory/calendar content saved after the pairing moment, • Deleting unused, unpaired accounts after a set period (storage limitation) and sending a notice before deletion, • Cryptographic encryption of time-locked letters and preserving their confidentiality until the chosen unlock date, • Displaying shared date locations on map with convenient routing, • Delivering timely push notifications for medicine schedules, cycle updates, anniversaries, and unlocked letters, • Provisioning CoupleSky Premium entitlements so that a single subscription covers both partners, • Maintaining network and information security against cyber attacks and brute-force attempts.

4. PARTIES TO WHOM PERSONAL DATA IS TRANSFERRED

Your personal data is never sold to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Data is shared strictly to perform our services with the following vetted providers, some of which are located internationally: • Supabase Inc.: Secure PostgreSQL database, encrypted cloud storage, and authentication infrastructure (data is hosted in the Frankfurt / Germany region), • RevenueCat Inc.: In-app purchases, subscription entitlement verification, and receipt validation, • Google Cloud / Firebase, Apple (APNs) & Expo Notifications: Delivery of push notifications to your device (notification text may include health details such as medicine name or cycle status that your partner shares, and passes through these services), • Google LLC (AdMob & Google Maps): Map services and non-personalized advertising, • Apple Inc. & Google LLC: Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google authentication services; on-device address search (the address text you type when choosing a location), • Expo (Expo Updates / EAS): Distribution of app updates; your IP address, platform and app version are sent when checking for updates, • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): Used as a fallback when the device's own address search returns nothing; only the text you type in the address search box and your IP address are sent, • Unsplash: Sample images shown in explore (demo) mode; your IP address is visible while the images load, • Competent Public Authorities: In response to lawful court orders or binding legal obligations. Basis for transfers abroad: Transfers to providers located abroad are made under Article 9 of KVKK, relying on an adequacy decision of the Personal Data Protection Board, or, where there is none, on the appropriate safeguards listed in Article 9 (standard contract, binding corporate rules, etc.) or other cases provided by law. You can ask which basis is used for which provider at support@couplesky.app.

5. DATA COLLECTION METHODS AND LEGAL GROUNDS

Your data is collected electronically via app UI inputs, camera/gallery permissions, device sensors, and notification permissions. Legal grounds include: • Contractual Necessity: Performance of the User Agreement and core service delivery, • Legitimate Interests: Enhancing app security, preventing fraud, and optimizing stability without overriding fundamental user rights, • Explicit Consent (KVKK Art. 6/2): For health-related cycle/medicine data, given with a separate tick box when you first use the feature, apart from the terms acceptance at sign-up. Location depends on your granting the device location permission and choosing to use the related feature.

6. DATA SECURITY AND ENCRYPTION (AES-256)

CoupleSky writes your daily notes and letters to the database encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is derived inside the app from a random, couple-specific value kept on the server. This is therefore not end-to-end encryption: as the party operating the service we are technically able to access this content, and we do not use it for any other purpose, including marketing. Access is limited by Row-Level Security (RLS) rules to authenticated members of the relevant couple. Your shared photos are kept in private storage and displayed only through time-limited, signed links; your profile photo (avatar), however, is kept in public storage at an unguessable address and can be viewed by anyone who has that address. All traffic is encrypted with TLS.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION (RIGHT TO BE FORGOTTEN)

You can request deletion of your account in Profile > Account Management & Danger Zone. The request waits 7 days; signing in with the same account during this period cancels the request automatically and you are informed. A reminder notification is sent 3 days and 1 day before the deletion day so you can still cancel. After 7 days everything you created, uploaded or wrote (profile details, photos, journals, letters, notes, special days, map pins, medicine and cycle records and content in your star) is permanently deleted from our servers. Your partner's account is not deleted; content your partner created remains available only to your partner. The shared star and the memory planets inside it are deleted regardless of who created them; automatically created anniversary entries are also deleted. Your partner's pairing code is renewed so your partner can pair again. Your partner is informed in the app and by notification about the request, reminders 3 days and 1 day before deletion, withdrawal and deletion. If both sides requested deletion, each account is deleted on its own 7th day; with the last account, everything remaining that belongs to the couple is permanently deleted. Backups are destroyed automatically within 30 days at the latest. Under GDPR Article 17 and applicable data protection legislation, the statutory right to delete your personal data, uploaded photos, and letters cannot be conditioned upon an active paid subscription. Users may permanently delete their own content or terminate their accounts at any time, even on the free tier (for letters already delivered to the partner, the partner-approved deletion rules apply). Retention periods: • Account, profile and content: until your account is deleted (deleted 7 days after the request). Content your partner created belongs to your partner as long as their account exists, even if you delete yours, • Unused accounts: New accounts that never paired are deleted 30 days after last use; accounts left alone after a couple ended are deleted 60 days after last use. Accounts with an active subscription are not deleted; they are deleted 60 days after the subscription ends. Paired accounts are never deleted for this reason. A notification is sent about 7 days and 1 day before deletion (it may not reach you if notifications are off), • Earlier data and new partners: In a new pairing your new partner only sees data saved after the pairing moment; data from your earlier relationship is not shown to your new partner and stays only in your access (you can delete it any time), • Star registration and coordinate: retained for the couple during a 14-day Stellar Hibernation (Grace Period) following subscription expiration; if not renewed within 14 days, the coordinate is released to the public catalog for other couples to claim. If your partner deletes their account, the star and the memory planets inside it are deleted without waiting for the hibernation/vault period, • Star memory vault and planet data: kept in the system vault for 90 days after the coordinate is released; if you subscribe again and claim a star in that time, the memory planets move to it, • Media storage purge: if the vault period (90 days after the coordinate is released) ends without renewal, full-size photo files, memory and planet images and profile photos are permanently deleted. Small previews in the photo archive and text records (memories, planets, letters, notes) are kept; a photo record without a small preview is deleted. The purge date is shown in the app under Profile > Premium; an app notification is sent about 7 days and 1 day before the purge (it may not reach you if notifications are off), • Error logs: at most 30 days, • Sign-in and password-reset attempt records (including IP address): at most 1 day, • Records that prevent duplicate notifications: 14 days, • In-app account notices shown to your partner: 30 days after they are seen, • Push notification token: for the life of your session and account (after a deletion request, only until the deletion day, for the deletion reminder), • Records held by third-party providers (e.g. RevenueCat, Expo, Google): subject to the provider's own retention policies.

8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER GDPR AND DATA PROTECTION LAWS

You have the right at any time to: a) Inquire whether your personal data is being processed, b) Request information regarding the processing of your data, c) Understand the purpose of processing and verify compliance, d) Know third parties to whom data has been transferred domestically or abroad, e) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, f) Request deletion or erasure of your personal data under statutory conditions, g) Request notification of corrections and deletions to third-party recipients, h) Object to decisions made solely by automated systems that cause an adverse legal effect, i) Seek compensation if you suffer damages due to unlawful processing. To exercise your rights, you can reach us through in-app support or directly via email at support@couplesky.app.
