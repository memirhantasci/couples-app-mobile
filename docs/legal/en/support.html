How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account Management & Danger Zone and choose "Delete My Account and All Data". The request is held for 7 days; you can cancel by signing in during that time. After that, your account and all your data are permanently deleted.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play. iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
