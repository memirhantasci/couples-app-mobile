Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 2026

Detailed guidelines on our data collection, processing, and protection principles, and how your couple privacy is strictly preserved.

1. INTRODUCTION AND OUR COMMITMENT

CoupleSky is designed for partners to cherish their most precious memories, letters, and daily moments within a protected private digital space. User privacy is our core foundation. Your personal data is never sold to third parties or traded with advertising brokers.

2. COLLECTED INFORMATION AND USAGE SCOPE

• Account Data: Name, username, email address, and securely hashed passwords provided during sign up. • Relationship Content: Photos, captions, time-capsule letters, and calendar notes. This content is exclusively accessible to you and your paired partner. • Cycle and Health Data: Menstrual start dates and medication hours. Used solely to provide cycle predictions and timely reminders for you and your partner. • Location Coordinates: Obtained only when you explicitly select a meeting point on the map or geotag a photo; no persistent background tracking is ever conducted. • Third-Party Infrastructure: Google AdMob, Google Maps, Apple/Google Sign-In, Supabase, RevenueCat, and Expo. For details, refer to Section 4 of our Clarification Text.

3. SECURITY AND CRYPTOGRAPHIC ENCRYPTION

All network traffic is encrypted via TLS/SSL protocols. Private letters and journal entries are encrypted before persistent storage. Shared photos are kept in private storage; your profile photo (avatar) is kept in public storage at an unguessable address and can be viewed by anyone who has that address. Session tokens reside in secure device storage; securing your device with biometric or passcode locks is strongly advised.

4. COOKIES AND UNIQUE DEVICE IDENTIFIER

To prevent brute-force attacks and unauthorized multi-device logins, a randomly generated UUID device identifier is utilized. When logging in from an unrecognized device, one-time verification is prompted to safeguard your account.

5. ACCOUNT ERASURE AND DATA PURGE (GDPR & CCPA COMPLIANT)

You may request account deletion at any time under Profile > Account Management & Danger Zone. Requests are subject to a 7-day cancellation window. Following the 7-day period, all account data, photos, and linked memories are irreversibly removed from active servers. Your paired partner is informed through an in-app explanation and a notification when you request deletion, cancel it, or it is carried out, and shared data is deleted for both sides.

6. AGE RESTRICTION

CoupleSky is not intended for individuals under 16 years of age. If we learn that personal data of a minor under 16 has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

7. POLICY UPDATES

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal requirements. Material updates will be highlighted via in-app notifications.
