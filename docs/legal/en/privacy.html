Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 2026

Detailed guidelines on our data collection, processing, and protection principles, and how your couple privacy is strictly preserved.

1. INTRODUCTION AND OUR COMMITMENT

CoupleSky is designed for partners to cherish their most precious memories, letters, and daily moments within a protected private digital space. User privacy is our core foundation. Your personal data is never sold to third parties or traded with advertising brokers.

2. COLLECTED INFORMATION AND USAGE SCOPE

• Account Data: Name, username, email address, and securely hashed passwords provided during sign up. • Relationship Content: Photos, captions, time-capsule letters, and calendar notes. This content is exclusively accessible to you and your paired partner. In a new pairing your partner only sees content saved after the pairing moment; content from an earlier relationship is not shown to your new partner. • Stars (Sky): Your star's name and your couple's initials are visible to other users and to visitors trying the app without an account. Your first name is shown only to couples you have a star bond with and, if you explicitly allow it under Certificate > Our Names & Photos, to everyone. Your profile photo (Premium) is shown on your star card to everyone only if you separately allow it in the same section; it is no longer shown when Premium ends or when you remove the photo. Each person gives their own consent (your partner cannot turn it on for you), and you can turn it off at any time. Your last name, username, and email address are never shown. Inappropriate star names, names, and photos can be reported in the app. • Cycle and Health Data: Menstrual start dates and medication hours. Used solely to provide cycle predictions and timely reminders for you and your partner. • Location Coordinates: Obtained only when you explicitly select a meeting point on the map or geotag a photo; no persistent background tracking is ever conducted. • Usage time: The last time your account was used. Kept only so unused, unpaired accounts can be deleted (Section 5). • Error and Security Logs: When the app errors, the error message, technical stack trace, app version and platform (at most 30 days); IP address used to rate-limit sign-in attempts (at most 1 day). Your content is not written to these logs. • Third-Party Infrastructure: Google AdMob, Google Maps, Apple/Google Sign-In, Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo (notifications and app updates), OpenStreetMap Nominatim (fallback address search) and Unsplash (sample images in explore mode). For details and information on transfers abroad, refer to Section 4 of our Clarification Text.

3. SECURITY AND CRYPTOGRAPHIC ENCRYPTION

All network traffic is encrypted via TLS/SSL protocols. Private letters and journal entries are encrypted before persistent storage. Shared photos are kept in private storage; your profile photo (avatar) is kept in public storage at an unguessable address and can be viewed by anyone who has that address. Session tokens reside in secure device storage; securing your device with biometric or passcode locks is strongly advised.

4. COOKIES AND UNIQUE DEVICE IDENTIFIER

To prevent brute-force attacks and unauthorized multi-device logins, a randomly generated UUID device identifier is utilized. When logging in from an unrecognized device, one-time verification is prompted to safeguard your account. In the friend invite program, the device identifiers of the inviting and invited couples are compared on our server to stop rewards being earned with accounts created on the same device; this comparison is not used for any other purpose.

5. ACCOUNT ERASURE AND DATA PURGE (GDPR & CCPA COMPLIANT)

You can request deletion of your account in Profile > Account Management & Danger Zone. The request waits 7 days; signing in with the same account during this period cancels the request automatically and you are informed. A reminder notification is sent 3 days and 1 day before the deletion day so you can still cancel. After 7 days everything you created, uploaded or wrote (profile details, photos, journals, letters, notes, special days, map pins, medicine and cycle records and content in your star) is permanently deleted from our servers. Your partner's account is not deleted; content your partner created remains available only to your partner. The shared star and the memory planets inside it are deleted regardless of who created them; automatically created anniversary entries are also deleted. Your partner's pairing code is renewed so your partner can pair again. Your partner is informed in the app and by notification about the request, reminders 3 days and 1 day before deletion, withdrawal and deletion. If both sides requested deletion, each account is deleted on its own 7th day; with the last account, everything remaining that belongs to the couple is permanently deleted. Backups are destroyed automatically within 30 days at the latest. Unused accounts: New accounts that never paired are deleted 30 days after last use; accounts left alone after a couple ended are deleted 60 days after last use. Accounts with an active subscription are not deleted; they are deleted 60 days after the subscription ends. Paired accounts are never deleted for this reason. A notification is sent about 7 days and 1 day before deletion (it may not reach you if notifications are off). Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple/Google subscription; cancel it separately in the store account. Your right to delete your own content does not depend on Premium, including after your subscription ends: you can delete the photos, special days and memory planets you added without Premium and send deletion requests for letters (the partner-approved deletion rules for letters apply). When Premium ends, the star enters a 14-day Stellar Hibernation; if not renewed, the star coordinate is released and memory planets and memories are kept in the vault for 90 days. If the vault period also ends without renewal, full-size photo files and profile photos are deleted from storage; small photo previews and text records are kept. The purge date is shown in the app under Profile > Premium. If your partner deletes their account, the star and planets are deleted without entering this process.

6. AGE RESTRICTION

CoupleSky is not intended for individuals under 16 years of age. If we learn that personal data of a minor under 16 has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

7. POLICY UPDATES

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal requirements. Material updates will be highlighted via in-app notifications.
