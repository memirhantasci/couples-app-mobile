2. SERVICE SCOPE AND COUPLE PAIRING

CoupleSky provides a private platform enabling two partners to connect via a unique pairing code to share memories, calendars, photographs, health tracking, and time-capsule letters. You must only share your pairing code with your trusted partner. In a new pairing the partner only sees content saved after the pairing moment. When a couple's relationship ends (for example your partner deletes their account), the shared star and its planets are deleted and content you created stays in your account.
