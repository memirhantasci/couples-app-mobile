Subscription & Refund Terms

App Store & Google Play · Last updated: October 2026

CoupleSky Premium subscription features, free trial duration (if defined), auto-renewal rules, cancellation steps, and statutory refund policies.

1. COUPLES PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTION & SHARED COVERAGE

CoupleSky Premium operates on a shared subscription model: when one partner subscribes, both partners unlock all Premium features instantly. Premium benefits include: • Unlimited high-resolution photo uploads and shared cloud archive, • Advanced cycle tracking and synchronized health notifications, • Unlimited time-capsule letters with future scheduled unlocks, • Memory timeline with interactive anniversary countdowns.

2. FREE TRIAL PERIOD

First-time subscribers may be eligible for a promotional free trial as displayed in the store paywall. No charges are billed during the trial. If the subscription is not canceled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the trial period, it will automatically convert into a paid recurring subscription.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND BILLING (STANDARD STORE EULA)

• Payment is charged to your Apple ID / Google Play Account upon confirmation of purchase. • Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle (monthly or annual plan). • Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period at the then-applicable renewal rate.

4. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION PROCEDURE

You can cancel your subscription at any time without cancellation fees. Cancellations must be performed through your store account settings rather than within the mobile app: • iOS (Apple App Store): Go to iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > CoupleSky > Cancel Subscription. • Android (Google Play Store): Open Google Play Store > Profile Icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > CoupleSky > Cancel Subscription. Upon cancellation, you will retain access to all Premium features until the conclusion of your active billing period.

5. RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL AND REFUND POLICY

Under applicable digital consumer regulations, statutory withdrawal rights do not apply once digital content or services have commenced with the consumer's consent. Refund requests for accidental or duplicate charges are administered directly by Apple Support (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play Customer Support in accordance with store refund policies.
